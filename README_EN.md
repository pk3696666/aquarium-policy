## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: Why does the app request "Camera" permission?
* **Answer**: Our "Test Strip Camera Scanner" feature requires camera access to capture your water test strips. This allows the app to automatically pre-fill key values like pH, NO2, and NO3, saving you from manual typing.
* **Privacy Assurance**: We take privacy seriously. The camera is only used locally to analyze colors in real-time. **No images are saved to your photo library or uploaded to any external server.**

### Q2: What should I do if the camera scan is not 100% accurate?
* **Answer**: Ambient lighting (such as yellow light or dark environments) can affect color recognition. To ensure accuracy, AquaMaster features an intuitive **adjustment picker** on the scan result page. You can easily tweak any value manually to match your physical color card before saving.

### Q3: Is my aquarium data synced to the cloud?
* **Answer**: **Yes!** AquaMaster fully supports Apple's **iCloud CloudKit Sync** technology. As long as your device is signed into the same Apple ID and has iCloud permissions enabled for the app, all your aquarium data, logs, and routines will automatically and securely sync across your iOS devices (e.g., iPhone and iPad)—no manual backups required.
