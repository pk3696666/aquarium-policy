AquaMaster: Aquarium Log Book - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 17, 2026

Welcome to "AquaMaster: Aquarium Log Book" (also known as "水族大師日誌 - 魚缸換水提醒與養魚紀錄", hereinafter referred to as "the App"). We highly value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your data.

🔒 Core Privacy Commitment

This App operates on a local storage and secure personal iCloud architecture. All aquarium logs and water quality data are stored on your local device or your personal iCloud space via Apple's SwiftData technology. The developer of this App does not run or own any third-party servers, and has no access to, nor stores or discloses your data to any third party.

1. Device Permissions Usage

📷 Camera Permission

When you use the "Test Strip Camera Scanner" feature, the App will request access to your camera.
Our Promise: The camera is strictly used to assist you in capturing the color status of your water test strips for local calibration and pre-filling. The captured images are only processed in temporary memory for analysis and are never saved to your photo library, nor uploaded to any external server or shared with third parties.

2. Collection and Use of Personal Information

This App does not require users to register an account, and does not collect any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number). All data remains securely stored on your local device and your personal iCloud account.

3. Cloud Storage and Synchronization

The App supports Apple's iCloud Service (CloudKit) to enable automatic data synchronization across your multiple iOS devices (e.g., iPhone and iPad). This synchronization process occurs entirely within Apple's secure ecosystem. The developer does not have access to your iCloud container data.

4. Third-Party Services and SDKs

The App may utilize native Apple frameworks (such as WeatherKit to retrieve local weather data for correlating weather with water parameter trends). These services adhere to Apple's standard privacy policies. The App does not collect any data through these third-party services.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective immediately when they are posted on this page.
